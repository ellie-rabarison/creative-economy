# Data sources

## World Bank - World Development Indicators (WDI)
- URL: https://databank.worldbank.org/source/world-development-indicators
- Downloaded: 2026-11-04
- Series: Individuals using the Internet (% of population); Logistics performance
  index: Overall; Gini index; Charges for the use of intellectual property, receipts
  (BoP, current US$); Population, total; Merchandise exports (current US$);
  Merchandise imports (current US$)
- Countries: Madagascar, Mauritius, Seychelles, Comoros + aggregates: World,
  Sub-Saharan Africa, Small states, Least developed countries (UN classification)
- Years: 2015–2025
- Files: data/raw/wdi/wdi_data.csv, data/raw/wdi/wdi_metadata.csv
- Notes: Latest year varies by country and indicator (full table in outputs/).

## World Bank – Worldwide Governance Indicators (WGI)
- URL: https://databank.worldbank.org/source/worldwide-governance-indicators#
- Downloaded: 2026-11-04
- Series: Rule of Law – Governance estimate; Government Effectiveness – Governance estimate
- Countries: Madagascar, Mauritius, Seychelles, Comoros (no aggregates needed:
  estimates are centred on a world average of 0)
- Years: 2015–2025
- Files: data/raw/wgi/wgi_data.csv, data/raw/wgi/wgi_metadata.csv
- Notes: "Governance estimate" (approx. −2.5 to +2.5) used rather than the 0–100
  "Governance score", as in the CEDM methodology (WIPO Philippines report, p. 83)
