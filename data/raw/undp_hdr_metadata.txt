# UNDP Human Development Report (HDR) – composite indices time series

## What it is
Bulk file of UNDP's composite indices and their components (HDI, GII, and
others) for all countries and regional aggregates. Published with the
Human Development Report 2025.

## Source
- Publisher: United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), Human Development Report Office
- Download page: https://hdr.undp.org/data-center/documentation-and-downloads
- Technical notes (methodology): https://hdr.undp.org/sites/default/files/2025_HDR/HDR25_Technical_Notes.pdf
- Downloaded: 2026-11-04
- File: data/raw/undp_hdr_data.csv
