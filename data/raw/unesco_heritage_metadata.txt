
# UNESCO heritage entries – Comoros, Madagascar, Mauritius, Seychelles

## What it is
Manually  compiled list of UNESCO World Heritage sites and Intangible Cultural
Heritage (ICH) elements for the four islands. One row per entry.

## How it was compiled
- Sources: UNESCO World Heritage Centre (whc.unesco.org, States Parties pages)
  and UNESCO ICH (ich.unesco.org, country pages)
- Compiled by: Ellie Rabarison, 2026-11-04
- Each entry checked on the official UNESCO page

## Columns
- country / iso3: country name and ISO 3-letter code
- list: "World Heritage" or "Intangible Cultural Heritage"
- name: name of the site or element 
- category: Cultural / Natural / Mixed (WH),
  Representative List / Urgent Safeguarding / Good Safeguarding Practices (ICH)
- inscription_year: year of inscription
- shared_with: other countries for multinational entries

## Choices and limitations
- Shared (multinational) ICH entries counted for each country involved
- Tentative lists not included
- Manual entry: may contain transcription errors; verify against UNESCO pages
- Counts measure recognition, not the size or value of cultural heritage
- Rainforests of the Atsinanana: on the List of World Heritage in Danger 2010–2025, removed in 2026 (per UNESCO page, checked 2026-11-04).
